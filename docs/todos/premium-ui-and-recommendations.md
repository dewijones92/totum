# "A nicer/sexy UI … video recommendations … sexy and triple A"

Dewi, 2026-10-06, while the tap-speed work was running. To be shaped in a conversation before anything
is built: what "triple A" means to him, which screens first, and what recommendations should draw on.

## Starting points for that conversation (not decisions)

- Look and feel: motion and transitions (shared-element player expand, scroll physics), richer artwork
  (large hero tiles, blurred backdrops), typography scale, and how far to push the brand palette in
  `docs/features/brand.md` and `visual-language.md`. Compare against apps he likes, with screenshots.
- Recommendations: what exists today (YouTube home feed, related videos) versus local signals
  (watch history, finish rate, skips, channels he never plays), and whether podcasts get the same.
  One recommendation seam for both pillars (the project's unified-seam law).
- Measure before and after: a recommendation that is never tapped is noise; log impressions and taps.
