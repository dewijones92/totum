# Choose audio, video (and quality) or both when downloading

On download, a sheet: 🎧 Audio · 🎬 Video (best / 1080p / 720p / 480p) · Both, with a "remember for next time" tick; long-press always asks again (Dewi's choice). Today the choice is audio or full video, quality is always best.
