# A ⭐ for any item

Star any item (video, episode, song, torrent file) from its menu or the player; a ⭐ Starred list in Library with sort/filter/shuffle; local and in the Drive backup; music keeps writing to YouTube Music too (Dewi's choice).

Decided 2026-10-23 (Dewi): starring a YouTube video also **Likes it on the account** (unstarring removes the Like),
and the Starred list **merges the account's existing Liked videos** with local stars; podcasts and torrents are local
only; songs keep writing to YouTube Music.
